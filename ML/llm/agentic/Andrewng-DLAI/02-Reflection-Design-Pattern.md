Instead of using llm for reflection, grading with a rubric gives more consistent results

>A quality rubric is ==a scoring guide used to evaluate the quality of work by listing specific criteria and describing different levels of achievement for each==. It serves as a transparent framework for assessment, helping both teachers and students by defining expectations for an assignment and providing a consistent, objective way to grade and improve work.
