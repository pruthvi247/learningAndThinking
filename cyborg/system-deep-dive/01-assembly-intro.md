Only three things are needed to understand how CPUs work. Casey believes that knowing them means you’ll be able to tell from any CPU announcement roughly how well it performs. Those three pillars of understanding:

How data moves in and out: load/store units and L1–L3 caches

How instructions flow through the pipes: branch prediction, i-cache

Execution unit scheduling: raw throughput per operation type
