## Perplexity
Here are the top five high-quality resources to start your ethical hacking journey as an experienced platform engineer, covering books, YouTube channels, and practical learning platforms:

## 1. Books

- **Hacking: The Art of Exploitation** by Jon Erickson  
    This book blends core concepts of C programming with hacking techniques, ideal for understanding exploit development and systems hacking fundamentals.
    
- **Penetration Testing: A Hands-On Introduction to Hacking** by Georgia Weidman  
    A practical guide focusing on hands-on labs, vulnerability assessment, and real attack scenarios with tools like Metasploit and Burp Suite.
    
- **The Web Application Hacker’s Handbook** by Dafydd Stuttard and Marcus Pinto  
    Deep dive into web app security and exploitation techniques with modern web technology context.
    

## 2. YouTube Channels

- **The Cyber Mentor**  
    Structured courses and tutorials covering ethical hacking fundamentals, OSINT, and security tool usage with clear explanations and hands-on demos.
    
- **Hak5**  
    Well-known channel featuring lots of practical hacking tool demos, physical security testing, and contemporary cybersecurity topics.
    

## 3. Online Platforms & Labs

- **TryHackMe**  
    Interactive, gamified cybersecurity labs beginning from beginner to advanced levels, excellent for practical learning and reinforcement.
    
- **HackTheBox**  
    Realistic penetration testing challenges and CTFs for progressively advanced skill development.
    

## Additional Recommendations

- Coursera’s _IBM Ethical Hacking with Open Source Tools_ course for a structured curriculum blending theory and practice.
    
- Follow security news sources and CVE trackers to stay current.
    

These resources collectively offer conceptual grounding, hands-on skills, latest trends, and learning community support to accelerate your transition into ethical hacking at a professional level.
